# Anti-Anthropoid Lifecycle (Anti-Anthropoid Lifecycle Control Skill)

[English Version](./SKILL.en.md) | [中文版本](./SKILL.md)

---

This skill deploys hard sciences—**operations research, complex adaptive systems, cognitive neuroscience, and evolutionary game theory**—to systematically deconstruct and counter "anthropoid lifecycle control" rhetoric that exploits the axiom of "**doing what is appropriate for one's age**" to impose psychological oppression, choice deprivation, and rigid behavioral shaping.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Core Tenet: Purge Rebellious Posturing, Ground in First Principles
> Strictly avoid puerile, emotionally adversarial rhetoric (such as "I don't care what you say" or "none of your business").
> Our goal is not interpersonal confrontation, but rather **dismantling the very conceptual legitimacy of coercive rhetoric from the ground up through technical rationality and irrefutable empirical laws**.

## The Deconstruction Workflow

Follow these four sequential phases to analyze rhetoric and formulate dialectical responses:

### Phase 1: Ingestion & Trope Detection
1. Ingest target assertion (from social media, generational lectures, or corporate age-gatekeeping).
2. Execute the underlying automated analysis utility for signature screening:
   ```bash
   python3 skills/anti-anthropoid-lifecycle/scripts/deconstruct.py --text "<target_statement>"
   ```
- **Completion Criterion**: Precisely isolate whether the statement posits age-gate deadlines, synchronized pipeline coercion, or trial-and-error deprivation.

### Phase 2: Fallacy Topology Mapping
1. Consult the [Taxonomy & Deconstruction Rubric (TAXONOMY.en.md)](./TAXONOMY.en.md) to locate the core structural failure:
   - **Synchronous Pipeline Fallacy**: Forcing heterogeneous, high-order intelligent agents into an industrial-era 1D synchronous clock;
   - **Primate Biological Reductionism**: Weaponizing pre-modern survival/reproductive constraints to hold modern knowledge work and creative value hostage;
   - **Cybernetic Variance Suppression**: Manufacturing artificial temporal panic to extract low-cost intergenerational control rents.
- **Completion Criterion**: Categorize the coercive rhetoric into its designated TAXONOMY archetype and expose its false axioms.

### Phase 3: Scientific Dialectical Synthesis
Synthesize arguments by combining at least two of the following foundational disciplines:
1. **Complex Systems & Operations Research**:
   - Model the human life trajectory as an **Asynchronous Event-Driven State Machine**;
   - Demonstrate that forcing temporal lock-steps produces cascading deadlocks and severe economic deadweight loss.
2. **Cognitive Neuroscience & Neurobiology**:
   - Introduce the dual-trajectory model of Fluid vs. Crystallized Intelligence;
   - Cite synaptic and cortical lifelong neuroplasticity, refuting biological determinism.
3. **Evolutionary Dynamics & Cybernetics**:
   - Reveal that "doing what fits one's age" functions information-theoretically as a degenerate meme designed to suppress innovation variance for administrative convenience.
- **Completion Criterion**: Produce a structured, logically closed, scientifically rigorous refutation.

### Phase 4: Tone Audit & Delivery
1. Audit generated output:
   - Remove any trace of passive-aggressive snark, sarcasm, or reactive emotional venting;
   - Guarantee a composed, objective, dignified, and constructive posture that commands cognitive authority.
- **Completion Criterion**: Deliver a polished, schema-compliant response or knowledge extraction card.
