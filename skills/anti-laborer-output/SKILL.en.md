# anti-laborer-output

Standard operational execution skill for AI agents: systematically deconstruct "laborer mentality" (fetishizing mindless toil and inverting means and ends) and "anthropoid lifecycle control" (coercive age-stage determinism mandating "do what fits your age").

Grounded in the root virtue of **Predictability**, this skill governs the agent's **deterministic reasoning and anti-AI-trope generation workflow**. All underlying empirical citations reside in the external knowledge catalog ([`knowledge/index.en.md`](file:///mnt/hdd/zcode-on-the-move/Anti-Laborer-skills/knowledge/index.en.md)). Hardcoding domain concepts inside this skill is strictly prohibited. Concrete de-smelling rubrics and before/after pairs are disclosed in [`STYLE-GUIDE.en.md`](./STYLE-GUIDE.en.md).

---

## Leading Words & Stance

1. **`protected spans`**: Scientific models, statutory legal lines, and user survival constraints declared in Step 0 constitute an inviolable fidelity perimeter. No de-smelling or pruning operation may alter or dilute them.
2. **`posture purge`**: Ruthlessly eliminate condescending AI postures—performative empathy (e.g., *"I deeply understand your exhaustion"*), unsolicited identity flattery, and omniscient narrator commentary. Mature discourse relies strictly on objective laws, cost trade-offs, and clear boundaries.
3. **`grounded prose`**: Reject mechanical binary formulas (`not X, but Y`) and hollow philosophical sign-offs (`in conclusion / become the master of your destiny`). Ground statements in concrete subjects, active verbs, tangible cost accounting, and operational allocations of responsibility.
4. **`two-pass quality gate`**: Enforce Pass 1 (Fidelity Audit) and Pass 2 (Residual & Posture Audit) sequentially. Premature completion without verified compliance is forbidden.

### Step 0: Grill-Me

Before drafting, eliminate all unexamined assumptions regarding the user's real situation. If the user prompt is underspecified, the agent must issue structured questions paired with recommended answers (➡️):

- **The Frontier Questions Structure**:
  ```
  ❓ Q1 [Interlocutor & Hierarchy]: Who is the interlocutor? What direct financial dependence or institutional hierarchy exists between you? (e.g., parents / spouse / direct manager / HR / public internet stranger)
  ➡️ Recommended: [Agent inference based on context]
  ---
  ❓ Q2 [Baseline Runway & Fragility]: What is your realistic financial runway and downside tolerance? Are there non-negotiable obligations (mortgage, dependents, gap-year survival limits)?
  ➡️ Recommended: [Agent safety boundary recommendation]
  ---
  ❓ Q3 [Target Outcome]: What exact result do you want this discourse to produce? (Soft de-escalation / preserve long-term relationship / draw clear workplace boundaries / thorough public refutation)
  ➡️ Recommended: [Agent tactical recommendation]
  ```

- **Completion Criterion**: Variables Q1, Q2, and Q3 are explicitly settled and the decision frontier is completely cleared.
- **Fast-Path Bypass**: If the user's initial prompt explicitly specifies relationship, runway, and objective—or states *"give me the text directly without grilling"*—treat the frontier as pre-settled and advance directly to Step 1.

### Step 2: Calibrate Stance & Scope

Lock parameters based on Step 0 outputs:

- **Stance (Tone Intensity)**:
  - `minimal` (Gentle Deflection): For family and elders. Validates safety concerns, decouples anxiety from rigid timetables, prevents escalation.
  - `standard` (Rational Alignment): For workplace and peers. Counters toil with output ROI; counters ageism with human capital option value.
  - `aggressive` (Theoretical Demolition): For public debates and ideological critique. Deploys full cybernetic variance suppression and macroeconomic deadweight loss models.
- **Scope (Output Length)**:
  - `concise`: 1–3 punchy sentences (instant messaging / spoken reply).
  - `modular`: 3–5 bullet points with clear headings (email / formal memo).
  - `analytical`: Comprehensive structured essay (public op-ed / column rebuttal).

- **Completion Criterion**: Exactly one `(Stance, Scope)` pair is locked.

---

### Step 3: Grounded Positive Drafting

Draft the response following positive assertion principles. **Ban formulaic AI slop**:

1. **Isolate Legitimate Care from Dogmatic Coercion**: Validate genuine baseline concerns (avoiding destitution, preserving health), while isolating the rigid age-clock or mindless toil premise as an obsolete, ineffective mechanism.
2. **Deploy the Knowledge Anchor (Translated to Grounded Causality)**: Translate the scientific mechanism from Step 1 (e.g., real options waiting value, crystallized intelligence accumulation) into plain, robust real-world logic regarding costs, learning curves, and market realities.
3. **Lock Boundaries & Barbell Hedge**: Conclude with an affirmative, agency-reclaiming position (e.g., barbell strategy: securing baseline while exploring convex upside), defining clear boundaries and concrete next steps.

- **Positive Drafting Standards**:
  - Direct subject-verb sentence constructions;
  - Ban mechanical binary contrasts (`not X, but Y`);
  - Ban significance inflation (`not only... but also...`);
  - Ban empty philosophical summaries or inspirational platitudes.

- **Completion Criterion**: First draft fully covers all three layers without logical gaps or performative fluff.

### Step 4: Two-Pass Quality Gate

Before delivery, execute two internal verification passes, referencing [`STYLE-GUIDE.en.md`](./STYLE-GUIDE.en.md):

#### Pass 1: Fidelity Audit
- [ ] **`protected spans` Intact**: Are scientific theorems, user survival constraints (runway months, debt figures), and statutory baselines 100% preserved?
- [ ] **Rigor & Causality**: Zero hallucinated statistics, zero unverified generalities?
- [ ] **Clear Agency**: Are systemic responsibilities cleanly distinguished from personal actions?

#### Pass 2: Residual & Posture Audit
- [ ] **Posture Purge**: Zero performative empathy (*"I hear your struggle"*), zero flattery (*"You've grasped the true essence"*), zero narrator commentary (*"This profoundly illustrates"*);
- [ ] **Syntactic Slop Purged**: Zero mechanical binary contrasts (`not X, but Y`), zero formulaic escalations (`not only... but also...`);
- [ ] **Closure Slop Purged**: Zero empty summaries (`in conclusion / at the end of the day`), zero inspirational motivational quotes;
- [ ] **Human Cadence**: Does the phrasing read naturally as a real professional speaking under actual constraints?

- **Completion Criterion**: Both Pass 1 and Pass 2 achieve 100% check-off. Any detected defect triggers immediate in-place revision before output.
