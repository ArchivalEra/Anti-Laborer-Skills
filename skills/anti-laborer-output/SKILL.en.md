# anti-laborer-output

Deconstructs "laborer mentality" (fetishizing mindless toil) and "anthropoid lifecycle control" (chrononormative age coercion). Enforces deterministic reasoning, factual fidelity, and grounded prose without AI tropes.

All empirical concepts reside in external catalog ([`knowledge/index.en.md`](file:///mnt/hdd/zcode-on-the-move/Anti-Laborer-skills/knowledge/index.en.md)).

## Leading Words

- **`protected spans`**: Scientific theorems from `knowledge/`, statutory rights, and user survival constraints (savings runway, debt) are inviolable. Zero drift permitted.
- **`posture purge`**: Strip performative empathy ("I hear your struggle"), unsolicited flattery ("you grasp the essence"), and narrator commentary ("this reveals"). State objective trade-offs directly.
- **`grounded prose`**: Direct subject-verb causality. Ban binary contrast formulas (`not X, but Y`), significance inflation (`not only... but also...`), and hollow philosophical closures (`in conclusion / become the master of your destiny`).
- **`two-pass gate`**: Mandatory sequential audit (Pass 1 Fidelity + Pass 2 De-smell) before delivery.

## Execution Steps

### Step 0: Grill-Me
Settle missing context before drafting. If relationship, financial baseline, or goal is unspecified, prompt:
1. Interlocutor: identity and institutional hierarchy (parent, boss, HR, peer, stranger).
2. Runway: financial cushion (months of expenses, non-negotiable debt, dependents).
3. Goal: target effect (soft deflection, boundary enforcement, or public refutation).

Completion criterion: Interlocutor, runway, and goal variables are explicitly bound. (Bypass if already specified or if user requests direct output).

### Step 1: Classify & Retrieve
Query the [Symptom-to-Knowledge Diagnostic Routing Matrix](file:///mnt/hdd/zcode-on-the-move/Anti-Laborer-skills/knowledge/index.en.md) in the master index to match colloquial claims against target concepts and empirical counter-cases. Fallback to domain catalogs (`cognitive-bias/`, `anthropoid-lifecycle/`, `labor-system/`, `innovation-economics/`, `comparative-global-models/`).

Completion criterion: At least one concept file and empirical case extracted as the grounding anchor.

### Step 2: Calibrate Stance & Scope
- Stance: `minimal` (family; defuse anxiety without escalation), `standard` (workplace; align on output ROI and option value), `aggressive` (public debate; cybernetic variance suppression).
- Scope: `concise` (1-3 sentences), `modular` (3-5 bulleted points), `analytical` (structured essay).

Completion criterion: Exactly one (Stance, Scope) pair locked.

### Step 3: Grounded Drafting
Draft using active assertions:
1. Isolate care from dogma: acknowledge legitimate baseline concerns (health, liquidity), but disconnect the obsolete toil/age premise.
2. Translate science to reality: explain the retrieved mechanism (real options waiting value, crystallized intelligence) as practical market costs and learning curves.
3. Establish boundaries: close on concrete operational choices or barbell risk hedging.

Completion criterion: Draft contains concrete causality without binary formulas (`not X, but Y`) or inspirational platitudes.

### Step 4: Two-Pass Quality Gate

Pass 1 (Fidelity Audit):
- `protected spans` intact: theorems, user runway months, debt limits, and statutory baselines preserved.
- Causality verified: zero hallucinated statistics or unbacked generalities.
- System vs personal responsibility cleanly distinguished.

Pass 2 (De-Smell Audit):
- Posture purged: zero "I understand your pain", zero flattery, zero narrator explanations.
- Syntax clean: zero `not X, but Y`, zero `not only... but also...`.
- Closure clean: zero `in conclusion / ultimately / at the end of the day` sign-offs.

Completion criterion: 100% check-off across both passes. Detected defects trigger immediate in-place revision.

### Step 5: Delivery Contract
Output with zero introductory pleasantries or meta-commentary:
- Default: Final deployable text directly.
- Diagnosis Mode (`diagnosis-mode`): Structured card containing [Statement], [Fallacy Category], [Knowledge Anchor], [Logical Vulnerability], and [Grounded Response].
