# Domain Overview: Global Comparative Lifecourse Models

## 1. Domain Scope

This domain applies **comparative sociology** and **comparative policy analysis** to examine structural variance in lifecourse rigidity across different cultural and political-economic systems. Cross-national comparison demonstrates that coercive chrononormativity is a historically contingent institutional choice rather than a universal law of nature.

## 2. Epistemological and Policy Value

Cross-cultural comparison provides:
- **Proof of Plural Equilibria**: Demonstrates that multiple stable institutional equilibria exist, deconstructing the fatalistic "there is no alternative" narrative.
- **Empirical Counterfactuals**: Evidence confirms that high-mobility labor frameworks (e.g., Danish Flexicurity) do not induce economic disorder; rather, they foster superior total factor productivity and aggregate well-being.
