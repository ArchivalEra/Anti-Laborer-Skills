# The Statistical Literacy of the Post-90s Unmarried Headcount: The Layered Verdict on "100 Million Unmarried out of 170 Million" and Its Misappropriation Chain

## 0. Scope Restriction

This entry follows the repository axiom: the object of analysis is **utterance and statistical text**, not **human beings**. No evaluation of "people who do not marry," no profile claims about unmarried cohorts (see the Scope Restriction in [`RUBRIC.en.md`](../../skills/source-evaluator/RUBRIC.en.md)). Nor does it adjudicate "whether one should marry"—what it fixes is **the scope boundary of the number** and **the misappropriation chain**.

---

## 1. Core Verdict: one number, two fates

The claim "**170 million post-90s, about 100 million unmarried**" splits into two halves:

| Component | Verdict | Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"~100m unmarried" (point-in-time state)** | **Order of magnitude holds** | Census-7 cohort 178.2m × 57.8% unmarried (2020) ≈ **103m**; end-2024 sampling implies ~95-100m in non-married states (media-derived) |
| **"170m" (denominator)** | **Stale scope, not fabricated** | Census-7 measured 178.2m; "170m" comes from pre-Census-6 materials (the 2021 Fujian factcheck itself used 170m) and persisted |
| **Any conclusion drawn from "100m unmarried"** | **Each needs its own audit** | "Unmarried" is a **point-in-time state** (a reversible intermediate state of first-marriage deferral), not "non-marriage" (intent), not "line-ending extinction" (terminal claim). The misappropriation happens at this step |

**Core scope distinctions** (the entry's central teaching point):

```
Unmarried (point-in-time, registration/de-facto scope) ≠ Single (includes divorced/widowed/living alone)
        ≠ Non-marriage-ism (a stated preference) ≠ Marriageable-age unmarried (age-restricted)
```

No official body has ever published a "post-90s marriage rate" or any "100m post-90s will not marry" statistic—**the only officially comparable figures** are: Census-7 cohort 178.2m; yearbook-scope 2020 unmarried rate 57.8%; 2020 mean first-marriage age 28.67; 2024 registrations 6.106m couples.

---

## 2. Timeline and Data Base

- **First-marriage age**: 24.89 (2010) → 28.67 (2020, census long form), a 3.78-year deferral; Shanghai 2024: 30.1; Anhui 2021: M 31.89 / F 30.73 (local civil-affairs scopes).
- **Unmarried rates** (de-facto scope, cohabiting included): age-30 unmarried 14.56% (2013) → 29.97% (2023), doubled; age-35 falls to 11.19%—**this 11.19% is the boundary evidence between "deferral" and "lifetime non-marriage"**: the overwhelming majority of unmarried status is delay, not abandonment.
- **Registrations**: 13.469m couples (2013 peak) → 6.106m (2024); 2023 rebounded 12.4% (post-pandemic compensation), 2024 fell 20.5% again. First marriages: 23.86m persons (2013) → 10.52m (2022).
- **Sex structure**: age-30 unmarried 2023: M 38.87% / F 20.16%—male unmarried near double female; the "rural surplus men × urban surplus women" spatial mismatch is a real structure (cohort sex ratio ~110).

## 3. The Misappropriation Chain (sourced nodes)

The claim's **numeric layer is broadly true**; its propagation lives on misappropriation:

1. **2021-08 original form**: "170m post-90s, only 10m couples married, marriage rate under 10%"—debunked by the Fujian platform 2021-08-26: no such MCA statistic exists; 2018-2019 alone saw 20m+ marriages aged 20-29. **This version's number is false.**
2. **Corrected form**: "about 100m post-90s unmarried"—coincides with the official-scope derivation (103m), **numeric layer holds**, but still often attributed to "civil-affairs statistics" (a fabricated attribution).
3. **Scope slip**: unmarried → single → living alone (absorbing the "92m empty-nest youth" figure, whose original surveyor is unknown) → "non-marriage, non-childbearing" (2024-08: "Why have hundreds of millions of youth given up on marriage and children, willingly going extinct?"—"extinction" as a terminal claim with no data).
4. **Narrative grafting**: onto fertility narratives ("if they won't even marry, what children?" 2024-11) and national-crisis narratives; overseas coverage folds it alongside "mandatory social insurance" into a "universal pay cut" frame.
5. **Same-family arithmetic errors**: "post-80s death rate exceeds post-70s" (2025-02), "post-90s survival rate only 85%" (2026)—the same **two-sampling-systems subtraction** error family as "marriage rate under 10%" (Tencent Jiaozhen, 2026-09-03).

## 4. Structural Analysis and Criteria

### 4.1 Why true numbers deserve more vigilance than false ones
False numbers (10m couples) die to fact-checks; **true numbers (100m unmarried) + fabricated attribution ("civil-affairs statistics") + scope slip (unmarried → non-marriage → extinction)** cannot be debunked wholesale—the fact-checker must concede the number and correct only the scope, and scope corrections travel far worse than numbers. This is an upgraded case of [`availability-heuristic`](../cognitive-bias/availability-heuristic.en.md): **the availability anchor is upgraded from illusion to true value**, while the inference chain behind the anchor (point-in-time state → terminal claim) remains a switch.

### 4.2 Criteria
- **Scope test**: for any claim citing "100m post-90s unmarried," ask three things—denominator scope (Census-7 178.2m or the stale 170m)? definition of unmarried (point-in-time or lifetime)? age restriction (whole cohort or marriageable band)? Failure to answer marks `FLAG_STATISTICAL_SCOPE_SLIP`;
- **Point-in-time/terminal test**: any inference from point-in-time unmarried rates to terminal conclusions ("non-marriage, extinction, generational break") must explain the counter-evidence of age-35 unmarried falling to 11.19%; unexplainable, treat as unfalsifiable;
- **Attribution test**: anything attributed to "civil-affairs/official statistics" where no official scope exists (the MCA publishes only total registrations and age distributions) is marked `FLAG_RUMOR_RETOLD` (fabricated attribution).

### 4.3 Division of labor with adjacent concepts
- The "national crisis / fertility collapse" graft is handled (utterance-level) by [`macro-entity-parasitic-projection`](../cognitive-bias/macro-entity-parasitic-projection.en.md);
- "Marry at the right age" discipline belongs to [`social-clock-phenomenology`](./social-clock-phenomenology.en.md);
- This entry owns only **the scope literacy of the number and the misappropriation chain**.
