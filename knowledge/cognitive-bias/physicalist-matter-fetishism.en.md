### Typical Rhetorical Symptoms
- "Typing code is just an illusion; the moment the grid goes down, it's all useless."
- "Stop studying abstract algorithms; learn a craft like lathe machining or vehicle repair—craftsmanship feeds you everywhere."
- "Coders are just modern clerks; delivery riders actually see packages moved across kilometers—what does code actually produce?"

### Cognitive Pathology
This discourse stems from **Pre-Modern Naive Physicalism and Anti-Abstraction Bias**:
1. **Sensory Trap of Classical Mechanics**: Confining cognitive models to macroscopic, low-velocity Newtonian mass displacement (moving bricks, stir-frying, turning wrenches), completely blind to sub-micron atomic synthesis and abstract bit-level orchestration;
2. **Denial of Non-Rival Leverage**: Equating economic value strictly with physical rivalry (atoms), while dismissing information as "negative entropy" that provides astronomical multipliers to global material throughput.

## 2. Scientific Deconstruction & Governing Laws

### 2.1 Information Theory & Thermodynamics: Information as Negative Entropy
Claude Shannon and Erwin Schrödinger established the physical definition of order and value:
$$\Delta I = - \sum p_i \log_2 p_i$$
- Physical matter naturally degrades toward **maximum entropy (Second Law of Thermodynamics)**: disorder, mechanical friction, and wasted thermal dissipation.
- True production does not create or destroy atoms (Conservation of Mass); it **reconfigures high-entropy chaotic atoms into functional low-entropy topological structures**.
- Ten thousand freight trucks colliding haphazardly on highways represent "massive physical displacement", yet deliver negative net value. A routing algorithm (written as code) reduces fleet fuel burn by 40% while quintupling delivery throughput. **Value creation lies in entropy reduction, not mechanical friction**.

### 2.2 Romer's Endogenous Growth Theory: Non-Rivalrous Ideas
Nobel laureate Paul Romer formulated the mathematical engine of modern wealth:
- **Physical Objects (Atoms)**: Inherently **rivalrous**. A wrench, a vehicle, or human muscular stamina can only be utilized in one location at one time, governed by diminishing marginal returns ($MC > 0$).
- **Abstract Ideas & Code (Bits)**: Inherently **non-rivalrous**. An optimal compiler algorithm, concurrency lock, or OS kernel incurs near-zero marginal reproduction cost ($MC \to 0$), simultaneously coordinating billions of physical machines worldwide.

> **Economic Principle**:
> Relying purely on physical manual skill confines an individual to **a fragile linear income ceiling bounded by 24 hours per day**.
> Dismissing code as "intangible fluff" is equivalent to claiming that the control grid coordinating global power networks, satellite constellations, and maritime logistics is "an imaginary phantom."
